## Full description

Chits is a cozy place for everything on your mind. Write notes the way you text a friend, then turn them into plans when you're ready.

💬 Chat-style notes
Jot down a thought, a checklist or a voice note in a familiar chat. Add photos, videos, PDFs and files right in the conversation, then search or organize them later.

🗂 Kanban boards for everyday plans
Move any note onto a board with columns like To do, Doing and Done. Drag cards between columns, add subtasks and set reminders.

📌 Sticky notes on the Fridge, Desk, Cork Board and Wall
Pin notes to a Space and move them around like the real thing. Choose sticky notes, lined paper or a receipt.

☀️ Plan your day, then wrap it up
Plan My Day helps you pick a few little things for today. In the evening, Wrap Up My Day lets you look back at what got done.

⏰ Calendar and reminders
Give any note a date, a time and a reminder. See what's coming up this week at a glance.

✅ Checklists everywhere
Groceries, packing lists, moving day: tick things off inside a note or a card and watch your progress.

✨ More ways to think
• Connect related notes
• Sketch ideas on a Canvas
• Focus on one note at a time
• Search everything you've written

🔒 Private by design
No account and no sign-in. Chits works offline and keeps your notes on your device. Back up and restore whenever you like.

Chits is the friendly sticky-note mascot who keeps you company along the way.
